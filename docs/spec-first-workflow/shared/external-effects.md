# External Effects

## Read When

- Immediately before an authorized external write, purchase, destructive or
  irreversible effect, sensitive-data operation, or quota-bound action.

## Authority Envelope

The accepted authority owns scope, cost, security, and proof bounds. Inside
that envelope, choose live parameters such as region, equivalent host or size,
bounded retry route, and local or remote execution from current evidence. A
rejected route is no longer valid. Reopen authority only to exceed the
envelope, weaken required security or proof, or change scope or behavior.

Prompts, handoffs, artifacts, logs, and receipts carry only a secret locator or
environment-variable name. A credential observed outside its secret channel is
exposed: stop using it, suspend its external-effect authority, and require
rotation before reuse.

## Stop Rule

The action remains inside the accepted envelope and returns proof of the exact
effect. Otherwise stop before the effect and name the missing authority.

Before requesting authority, finish independent authorized preparation so the
user can review a concrete candidate, its business consequence, cost, and
recovery boundary. Ask about the missing consequence or budget, not which
technical mechanism to choose. Do not defer an outcome-defining business
question until after work that depends on its answer.
