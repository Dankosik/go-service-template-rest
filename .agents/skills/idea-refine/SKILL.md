# Idea Refine

Drive convergence by reconstructing the problem, affected users, desired outcome, core bet, MVP boundary, decisive assumptions, and material alternatives from the raw idea and available evidence. Disposition each alternative as selected, eliminated with evidence, or blocked by one named open decision. Use [the workflow router](../../../docs/spec-first-workflow.md) and load [the reference selector](references/index.md) only when one concrete product pressure can change the direction. Complete with one evidence-backed direction for `spec-first-brainstorming`, or the material decision that still prevents convergence.
