status: ready

Each row names the smallest boundary that can falsify one accepted behavior.
Unit tests own deterministic policy mechanics; real transports own attempt,
metadata-ordering, cancellation, and generated-client composition claims.

## Traceability and proof limits

- TD-1 through TD-6 prove the closed transport policy and every currently
  identified gRPC late-metadata route. Header-only unit tests are rejected
  because they cannot prove OpenTelemetry attempt parentage or grpc-go ordering.
- TD-7 and TD-8 prove repository composition and cancellation over real
  transports. They do not prove that Railway, a proxy, or a service mesh
  preserves the headers; that remains deployment-specific evidence.
- TD-9 proves supported generated-client seams without creating a false
  downstream contract. A real provider schema remains the implementation-time
  authority for its generated API.
- A broad green suite cannot replace TD-2, TD-5, or TD-6: those rows require
  controlled retry/configuration failure and per-attempt wire observations.
- The deterministic transparent-retry peer is deliberately implemented with
  already-pinned public `x/net/http2` and `hpack` APIs, not grpc-go internal
  packages. Failure to observe both attempts is a test failure, not an allowed
  proof downgrade.
- Correlation values, headers, and raw URLs are asserted absent from HTTP
  metric attributes in TD-1 and from gRPC metric attributes in TD-3; bounded
  static method/dependency attributes remain allowed.
- Documentation correctness in TD-9 is a root review procedure backed by the
  real generated compile proof; `git diff --check` proves formatting only.
