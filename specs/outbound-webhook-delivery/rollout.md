# Outbound webhook delivery — Rollout

status: ready

Affected graph: one runtime image and canonical `/migrate` -> the same image
with optional `/webhook-worker`; feature transaction -> PostgreSQL webhook
acceptance -> independent worker -> subscriber secret authority and external
HTTPS receiver. OUTBOX, INBOX, JOBS, MESSAGING, subscriber management, and
business-event semantics are unchanged.

Irreversible boundary: before the first webhook acceptance, service and worker
may roll back and the additive unused schema may remain. The first accepted
delivery starts a payload, retry, redrive, receiver-dedup, and audit horizon;
from then on the schema and a compatible worker must remain until every accepted
identity reaches its authoritative retention/privacy checkpoint. Production
`Down` is not rollback.

Completion: one concrete business mutation and complete webhook fan-out are
durably accepted together on the writer; the independently deployed worker is
ready and produces the accepted signed receiver attempt and local finality or
operator-actionable ambiguity; target receiver, PostgreSQL, and bounded
telemetry readbacks agree. Component health or deployment status alone cannot
complete the path.

The sequence and rollback boundary are Technical Design decisions. Test Design
has supplied each proof cell's exact scenario, command or bounded procedure,
environment/fixture, and terminal oracle, so this record is ready for Planning.
Nothing here authorizes migration, deployment, production emission, or
contraction.
