# Durable Background Jobs — Rollout

status: ready

Affected graph: one runtime image and canonical `/migrate` -> the same image
with `/jobs-worker`; API producer -> PostgreSQL acceptance rows -> jobs worker
attempts -> feature/downstream effect. OUTBOX, INBOX, MESSAGING, and their
deployables are unchanged.

Rollback boundary: first acceptance of a new revision starts its
behavior-changing/live-job horizon, but it remains executable by both N and the
pre-proven N-1 rollback image. This initial rollout has no accepted
incompatible-worker boundary. Irreversibility begins only in a later contract
release that removes an old definition, schema, or retained authority; Gate 6
forbids that here. Production `Down` migration is not a rollback action.

Completion: the concrete feature's business mutation and job acceptance are
atomic on the writer; the independently deployed worker is ready, performs the
admitted durable effect under attempt fencing, and records the accepted terminal
or operator-actionable outcome; target telemetry/readiness and authoritative
effect readback agree. Component health or provider deployment status alone is
insufficient.

This sequence is ready for Planning of the reusable generic pack. Gates 1 and
3-6 remain fail-closed for a concrete kind or production rollout until their
named adopter inputs and target receipts exist; readiness here neither supplies
those values nor authorizes deployment, contraction, migrations, or execution.
