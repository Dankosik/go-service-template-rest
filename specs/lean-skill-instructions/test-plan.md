# Lean skill instruction test plan
status: ready

## Proof boundary

The accepted current completion is Stage 1: rewrite every canonical skill, add
the implicit specialist router, extend structural and fixture-backed checks,
and keep all 29 specialists implicitly eligible. Stage 2 policy activation is
outside the current completion until an authorized runner, judge, and model
cost are available and the live activation gate passes.

Structural checks do not prove model behavior. Stage 1 therefore closes only
the catalog structure, instruction ownership, deterministic eval definitions,
harness isolation, and local integration gates. Root review of the complete
skill diff is the non-test proof for behavior-preserving instruction ownership.
Live Stage 1 comparison and Stage 2 activation remain explicitly unavailable
proof, not evidence inferred from green local checks.

The current environment also exposes no independent raw runtime-event artifact
that can prove `go-specialist-router` was selected implicitly. Fake adapters can
prove only the closed ABI's fail-closed validation. Before Stage 2, Technical
Design and Test Design must reopen against the authorized runtime, name its
independently inspectable native event artifact and verifier, and bind the
adapter's metadata mapping to that evidence. No self-declared
`provenance_source` can activate Stage 2.

Baseline roles are fixed and non-interchangeable: the future Stage 1 behavior
comparison uses pre-implementation commit
`9a1dc4fc01a628b593e9008ab2cdf895a8f8a02b`; `34d9776` is only the historical
size-report baseline with rename mappings; the future accepted Stage 1 commit
is exclusively the immutable Stage 2 activation baseline.

## Traceability disposition

- The adapter-content claim is owned by TD-01 because no structural or model
  sample can prove every deletion across the catalog.
- Universal structure, specialist compatibility, and eval-shape claims are
  owned by TD-02 through TD-05.
- Harness secrecy, authorization, routing-policy integrity, implicit
  provenance, and experiment validity are owned by TD-06 through TD-10.
- The unavailable behavioral boundary, unavailable native provenance, and
  future activation/rollback are owned by TD-09, TD-11, and TD-12; none may be
  reported as passed in Stage 1.
- Integrated repository readiness and final task-bundle cleanup are owned by
  TD-13 and TD-14 and remain narrower than live model-behavior proof.

## Stop and reopen

Planning may proceed when an independent QA review returns `PASS` and can map
every Stage 1 row to a task owner without choosing another oracle, metadata
policy, ABI field, or command. Reopen Technical Design if the checker cannot
derive triggerable skills or the explicit-only set, if snapshot sealing cannot
derive selected bundles, or if runtime provenance cannot be represented by
the closed ABI. Reopen Specification before removing an existing skill or
changing observable routing scope. Keep Stage 2 blocked until TD-11's external
inputs and the accepted Stage 1 commit exist and TD-09 has reopened Technical
Design/Test Design against an independently inspectable native runtime-event
artifact.
